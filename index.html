About SFI Dakshina Kannada

Empowering the student community of the coastal district to fight for educational justice and progressive values.

Our History

The Students' Federation of India (SFI) was formed in 1970, out of the need to unite the student community on a national scale to resist anti-student policies, commercialization, and privatization of education. Over the last five decades, SFI has grown into India's largest progressive student organisation, with millions of members.

In the Dakshina Kannada district, SFI has a rich legacy of leading struggles. Being an educational hub with hundreds of professional, degree, and pre-university colleges, Mangaluru and its surrounding taluks have witnessed historical student movements led by SFI demanding fair bus passes, well-equipped government hostels, transparent evaluation processes, and democratic representation within educational institutions.

1970

All India Foundation

SFI formed at the All India Student Conference in Thiruvananthapuram, presenting the slogan "Study and Struggle".

1980s

Establishing in Coastal Karnataka

Initiation of district units in Dakshina Kannada to represent students in government and private colleges.

Present

Leading Modern Struggles

Mobilizing students against national education policies, NEET corruption, and local student rights.

Our Ideology

These three words form the core ideology of SFI and are represented on our white flag with its five-pointed red star.

Independence

An education system free from imperialist influences and commercial interests. Promoting scientific temper and rational, progressive thinking among students.

Democracy

The right of students to organize, speak, and vote. SFI demands the restoration of democratic student union elections in all colleges and universities.

Socialism

Fighting for a society free from exploitation. We believe that quality education is not a commodity for sale, but a fundamental human right available to all, regardless of caste or economic status.

Organizational Structure

SFI operates on democratic centralism. Decisions are taken collectively after open discussions. Our structure ensures representation from the grassroots college level to the national executive.

Primary / Unit Committee

Formed within individual colleges and high schools. The direct link to the student body.

↓

Local / Taluk Committee

Coordinates unit activities in specific taluks (e.g., Mangaluru, Bantwal, Puttur, Sullia, Belthangady).

↓

District Committee (SFI DK)

The governing body for Dakshina Kannada district, directing the local taluk committees and student struggles.

↓

State Committee

Coordinates all district committees within Karnataka, headquartered in Bengaluru.

↓

Central Executive Committee (CEC)

The apex national body representing SFI across all states of India.

Student Rights & Bus Pass / Hostel FAQ

Everything you need to know about student bus passes, hostels, and college fee regulations in Dakshina Kannada.

How to apply for Seva Sindhu KSRTC Student Bus Pass in DK? +

Apply online on the official Seva Sindhu portal with your college admission receipt, Aadhaar number, and passport photo. Submit the printed acknowledgment form to your college office for verification. If your bus pass is delayed beyond 7 days, contact the SFI Student Helpline.

What are the eligibility rules for SC/ST & BCM Government Hostels? +

Students studying in PU, Degree, and Post-Graduate courses can apply through the SSP/State Social Welfare portal. Sanction is based on distance and annual family income criteria. SFI conducts hostel inspection drives across Mangaluru to ensure hygienic food and safety.

What to do if private/aided college management demands illegal donation or extra building fees? +

Under Karnataka Education Department rules, colleges cannot charge unapproved fees above the government prescribed fee structure. You can log an anonymous complaint on the SFI Help Desk portal or contact our district secretariat. SFI will represent your issue directly to the Joint Director of Collegiate Education.
